- ## Our project

1. A portable, fully mechanical tool that lifts a heavy or stuck manhole cover from above. One worker places a tripod over the cover, lowers folding arms through an access hole, locks them under the cover with an over-centre linkage and latch, and turns a T-handle. A worm gear and Acme lead screw raise the cover. There is no motor and no electronics.
   
2. The manhole cover must have a suitable access hole for this mechanism to work. The folded arms are inserted through the hole and then opened underneath the cover. Once the arms are securely locked, the handle is turned to lift the cover. The hole dimensions will be finalised during CAD design and prototype testing. Covers without a suitable opening are not compatible with the current design unless the lifting mechanism or cover interface is modified.

3. How It Works

Position: Place the tripod over the manhole cover.
Insert: Lower the folded arms through the access hole.
Lock: Open the arms underneath the cover and secure the locking mechanism.
Lift: Turn the T-handle to operate the worm gear and lead screw.
Lower: Reverse the handle, release the lock after unloading, and remove the tool.

## Parts and materials (prototype)

3D printed in PETG (frame, tubes, gearbox, handle), nylon PA12 (arms, cam,
collar, gears, links, latch) and TPU (feet, pads, grip). Steel hardware:
M4 bolts, dowel pins, rollers, 4 mm rod. T8 lead screw with brass nut.
