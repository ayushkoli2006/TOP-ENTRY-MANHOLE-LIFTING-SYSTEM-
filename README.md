- ## Our porject

A portable, fully mechanical tool that lifts a heavy or stuck manhole cover from above. One worker places a tripod over the cover, lowers folding arms through an access hole, locks them under the cover with an over-centre linkage and latch, and turns a T-handle. A worm gear and Acme lead screw raise the cover. There is no motor and no electronics.

## Parts and materials (prototype)

3D printed in PETG (frame, tubes, gearbox, handle), nylon PA12 (arms, cam,
collar, gears, links, latch) and TPU (feet, pads, grip). Steel hardware:
M4 bolts, dowel pins, rollers, 4 mm rod. T8 lead screw with brass nut.
